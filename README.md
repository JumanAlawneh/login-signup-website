## ✨ The Story Behind Luxe Bags

Luxury is more than what you carry — it's how you express yourself.

**Luxe Bags** is a luxury-inspired handbag showcase designed to bring sophistication and style into one elegant digital experience. Featuring a sleek black-and-gold aesthetic, the website invites visitors to explore a curated collection of handbags, discover product details, and find pieces that match their personal style.

Built with **HTML, CSS, and JavaScript**, this project combines fashion-inspired visuals with interactive front-end functionality.

## 🛍️ Shopping Bag Experience

The dedicated `cart.html` page introduces an improved shopping bag interface designed to complement the website's luxury aesthetic.

Visitors can:

- View handbags added from the home page or product details page.
- See product images, names, unit prices, and line totals.
- Increase or decrease item quantities.
- Remove individual items from the shopping bag.
- View the number of selected pieces.
- Review the subtotal and total.
- Return to the collection to continue browsing.
- See an empty-bag message when no products are selected.

The shopping bag and product pages share browser-side storage through `localStorage`, allowing cart data to remain available across the website's pages in the same browser.

## 🚀 How to Explore

1. Open the live website.
2. Browse the handbag collection.
3. Use the search feature to find a handbag.
4. Select **View Details** to explore a product.
5. Choose a quantity and try **Add to Shopping Bag**.
6. Open the shopping bag to review your selected items.
7. Increase or decrease quantities, or remove items.
8. Check the updated subtotal and total.
9. Save or remove handbags from your favorites.
10. Explore the login and sign-up interfaces.

To run the project locally, download or clone the repository and open the appropriate HTML page in your browser.
