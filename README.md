## ✨ The Story Behind Luxe Bags

Luxury is more than what you carry — it's how you express yourself.

**Luxe Bags** is a luxury-inspired handbag showcase designed to bring sophistication and style into one elegant digital experience.

Featuring a refined black-and-gold aesthetic, the website allows visitors to explore a curated handbag collection, view individual product details, search for their favorite styles, save favorites, and manage a shopping bag.

Built with **HTML5, CSS3, and JavaScript**, this project combines fashion-inspired visuals with interactive front-end functionality.

## 🛍️ Shopping Bag Experience

The dedicated `cart.html` page provides a shopping bag interface that complements the website's luxury aesthetic.

Visitors can:

- View handbags added from the collection or product details page.
- See product images, names, unit prices, and line totals.
- Increase or decrease item quantities.
- Remove individual items.
- View the number of selected pieces.
- Review the subtotal and total.
- Return to the collection to continue browsing.
- See an empty-bag message when no products are selected.

The shopping bag and product pages use browser-side `localStorage` to share cart data across pages in the same browser.

## 🚀 How to Explore

1. Open the live website.
2. Browse the handbag collection.
3. Use the search feature to find a handbag.
4. Select **View Details** to explore a product.
5. Add a handbag to your shopping bag.
6. Open the shopping bag to review selected items.
7. Increase or decrease quantities, or remove items.
8. Check the updated subtotal and total.
9. Save or remove handbags from your favorites.
10. Explore the login and sign-up interfaces.

### Run Locally

1. Clone or download the repository.
2. Open the project folder.
3. Open `index.html` or `home.html` in your browser.
